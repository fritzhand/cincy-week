About Brand Fusion #

Brand Fusion is a curated matchmaking event that connects Fortune 500 and Emerging brand leaders with innovative startups through structured 15-minute meetings. Since 2012, we've brought together the brightest minds in brand innovation and startup technology to forge partnerships that drive real business results.

Brands submit their toughest business challenges. Venture capitalists and accelerators nominate their most relevant startups. Our team personally curates each pairing, drawing on deep knowledge of both sides to create a full day of high-impact meetings.

Questions and answers #

All 19
What is Brand Fusion?

Brand Fusion is a curated matchmaking event that connects Fortune 500 and Emerging brand leaders with innovative startups through structured 15-minute meetings. Our team personally curates each pairing, drawing on deep knowledge of both sides to create a full day of high-impact meetings.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)

When is Brand Fusion 2026 Powered by Markup AI?

Brand Fusion 2026 Powered by Markup AI takes place over two days: a Welcome Dinner on Tuesday, October 6, and a full Matchmaking Day on Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)

Who should attend?

Brand Fusion is designed for brand-side marketing and innovation leaders, startup founders and executives, and venture capitalists / accelerator partners who nominate startups.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)

Where is the Welcome Dinner (Day 1)?

The Welcome Dinner is held at SparkHaus, 727 Madison Ave, Covington, KY. It's walkable from Hotel Covington.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)

Where is Matchmaking Day (Day 2)?

Matchmaking Day takes place at TQL Stadium, 1508 John St, Cincinnati, OH. Brand-startup meetings are held in the stadium's private suites, with meals and kickoff in the Tunnel Club.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)

How far apart are the venues?

SparkHaus and TQL Stadium are both within a short drive of Hotel Covington. SparkHaus is walkable from the hotel. TQL Stadium is approximately a 5-minute drive across the river.

Source: brandfusioncincy.com (opens in a new tab)
