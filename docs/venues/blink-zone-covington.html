0H10M1ke Light Installation

Details

0H10M1ke is a Brooklyn-based interdisciplinary artist known as “The One-Line Master,” recognized for creating more than 15,000 live portraits and light-based performances across cities including New York, Miami, Tokyo, Kyoto, and Berlin. Working at the intersection of drawing, performance, and light, he translates real-time gesture into luminous forms using a Wacom tablet and stylus, treating each mark as an immediate, unrepeatable response to the moment. His ongoing series L1ght Graff1t1 Make0vers transforms participants into vivid, reimagined identities through live digital light performance, blending street aesthetics, improvisation, and spectacle. Across international exhibitions at venues such as the Park Avenue Armory, the Guggenheim Museum, and the Museum of Graffiti, his work explores light as both medium and metaphor for connection, impermanence, and shared human presence.
