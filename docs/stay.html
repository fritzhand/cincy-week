How to read the cards

Walking times are estimates: the straight-line distance × 1.3, at 80 m a minute. Chips show minutes to Union Hall (StartupCincy Week hub), Art Week hub (1600 Race St) and Fountain Square; beyond a 45-minute walk they show the distance instead.
