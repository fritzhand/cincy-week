About StartupCincy Week #

StartupCincy Week 2026 marks a decade of backing founders who refused to play it safe, building companies that didn't ask permission, and proving Cincinnati is where bold bets belong.

StartupCincy Week 2026 is four days of bold ideas, meaningful connections, and the people building what’s next. Explore the sessions, find the conversations that matter to you, and start mapping out your week.

History · 11 notes
  1. 2016

    Precursor: Techstars FounderCon 2016 brought "More than 1,000 of the nation's top business builders" to Cincinnati, Oct. 18–20, 2016 — "the first time in six years that Techstars is hosting their annual innovation event in a location outside of their traditional network of startup hubs". (Not a StartupCincy Week.) (prnewswire.com (opens in a new tab))

  2. 2017

    First edition, "Techstars Startup Week Cincinnati, held Oct. 9-13 in Union Hall and other Downtown and Over-the-Rhine locations." Results reported by WCPO: 118 events (goal 60); attendance 4,500 (goal 3,000, counting people attending more than one event); $59,000 in sponsorship (goal $30,000); 1,441 unique names collected; 34% minority and 33% female participation; more than 4 million social impressions; guests from 50 cities, 17 states and five foreign countries. An attendee noted the week was free. (wcpo.com (opens in a new tab))

  3. 2017

    Origin story (2026 retelling): "What began as a grassroots movement and a Twitter hashtag" (#StartupCincy). Abby Grimm: "I think the first year of StartupCincy Week was maybe around 100 people, and this year we’re expecting 1500 people to come." (Conflicts with WCPO's 2017 attendance count of 4,500.) (soapboxmedia.com (opens in a new tab))

  4. 2022

    Sixth annual StartupCincy Week, October 10–14, 2022: "Union Hall welcomed over 1,000 people ... 35 events featuring over 150 speakers across four venues." Key partners: Alloy Development Co., Blue North, CincyTech, Cintrifuse, ECDI, Endeavor, Flywheel Social Enterprise Hub, Product Refinery, REDI Cincinnati, and Reversed Out Creative. (startupcincy.com (opens in a new tab))

  5. 2023

    StartupCincy Week 2023, October 23–26, theme "Homegrown Hustle": "we welcomed over 1,000 dreamers to Union Hall ... 45 events featuring over 100 unique speakers." Planning committees: Alloy, Aviatra Accelerators, Blue North, CincyTech, Cintrifuse, ECDI, Flywheel Social Enterprise Hub, gener8tor, Gratitude Collective, Miami University, and the University of Cincinnati. First formal partnership with the Over-The-Rhine Chamber of Commerce (Movers & Makers). (startupcincy.com (opens in a new tab))

  6. 2024

    Eighth annual StartupCincy Week, October 7–10, 2024, in Over-the-Rhine, "presented by Cintrifuse" and "sponsored by Fidelity Private Shares, P&G, and many other ecosystem partners". Tickets: general admission ($50), corporate admission ($200), VC Investor ($300). Daily themes: Monday whole community, Tuesday high-growth tech founders and investors, Wednesday non-tech and early-stage founders, Thursday the region (Chamber Expo, small business and CPG workshops). (cdomagazine.tech (opens in a new tab))

  7. 2025

    StartupCincy Week 2025, October 6–9, at Union Hall, Over-the-Rhine. New $200,000 live pitch competition, "Organized by the nonprofit Cintrifuse and powered by Connetic Ventures", with five finalists on Oct. 7 at Union Hall; judges Kirsten Moorefield (Cloverleaf), Somesh Saxena (Pantomath), Raf Garcia (Kernel). Organizers expected "more than 70 venture capital investors" and programming "shaped by more than 20 ecosystem partners". (moversmakers.org (opens in a new tab))

  8. 2025

    2025 day themes (leftover hidden text on startupcincyweek.com): MONDAY, OCT. 6: Kick off the week with founder stories, ecosystem content, and get plugged into everything Cincinnati has to offer. (Kick-Off & Keynote, Best of Demo Night, Founder Development); TUESDAY, OCT. 7: A deep dive into venture capital for startups scaling here in the Midwest. (Cintrifuse Annual Meeting, State of Venture Panel, Startup Pitch Competition); WEDNESDAY, OCT. 8: Calling all early-stage and non-tech founders, this day is all about getting started with confidence. (Speed Mentoring, Upskilling Workshops, Hard Rock Happy Hour); THURSDAY, OCT 9: Explore how our patchwork of businesses and collective action are making Cincinnati a great place to build, grow, and scale. (Small Biz & Creative Workshops, Food Truck Fair, Regional Chamber Expo) (startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab))

  9. 2025

    2025 Title Sponsors: P&G, Cintrifuse (with Cintrifuse Capital) and Fidelity Investments. The Startup Clubhouse was hosted at Somerhaus. StartupCincy.com "was launched in 2025 as what Grimm called a “digital front door” for the region’s startup ecosystem" (Soapbox). (startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab))

  10. 2026

    First-ever StartupCincy Awards, April 30, 2026, 5:30–7:30 pm at Union Hall: "This event kicks off StartupCincy’s 10th anniversary, honoring a decade of work and the people behind it." (luma.com (opens in a new tab))

  11. 2026

    10th StartupCincy Week, October 5–8, 2026, theme "Ten Years of Bold Bets": first-ever Student Pitch Competition and a full Student Day (Thursday); new city-building track; expected about 1,500 people. (soapboxmedia.com (opens in a new tab))

Who is StartupCincy Week for?

Honestly, everyone! From super seasoned founders and investors to folks just looking to get a little of that startup magic and inspo - there truly is something here for everyone. Our flexible programming allows you to write your own StartupCincy story and pick the path that makes the most sense for you!

Source: startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab)

Where can I park for StartupCincy Week at Union Hall?

We recommend two convenient parking options near Union Hall: Mercer Commons Garage, located at 1310 Vine Street, Cincinnati, OH 45202, or Washington Park Garage, located at 1230 Elm Street, Cincinnati, OH 45202.

Both garages are within a short walking distance to Union Hall and offer convenient access for attendees. Please note: we do not offer parking validation.

Source: startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab)

Can I attend StartupCincy Week virtually?

At this time, StartupCincy Week is a fully live and in-person event. We’ve found that the real-time energy field and human connections of this week simply can’t be replicated over a screen. Feeling is believing!

That said, we will be doing our best to provide attendees with as much supplemental content post-week as we can!

Source: startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab)

When will the agenda for StartupCincy Week be released?

The full agenda will be released around August. Be sure to subscribe to our newsletter so you’re the first to know when it drops, along with speaker announcements, session highlights, and more!

Source: startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab)

What food will be served at StartupCincy Week?

We’ve got you covered (and caffeinated)! Each day of StartupCincy Week, we’ll provide light bites and coffee for breakfast to help you start strong. We’re also hosting one community lunch during the week to bring everyone together over great food and conversation.

For other meals, we encourage you to explore the amazing restaurants in the Over-the-Rhine (OTR) neighborhood—home to some of Cincinnati’s best spots. We’ll even have exclusive discounts at a few of our favorite local restaurants for attendees to enjoy.

Source: startupcincyweek.com (opens in a new tab)

Volunteering is a great way to engage with the community. You can find information about volunteer opportunities here.
