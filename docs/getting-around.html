The Connector streetcar

The Connector is an electric-powered streetcar that operates along a 3.6-mile loop connecting Cincinnati's Over-the-Rhine and Banks neighborhoods via the Central Business District. It is free to ride.

Details

Hours (365 days a year, "core schedule with extended service hours for select events"): Monday through Friday 7:30 a.m.–11:30 p.m.; Saturday 8:30 a.m.–11:30 p.m.; Sunday 9:30 a.m.–9:30 p.m. "There are 18 stations along the route." Fare: "FARE FREE EVERY DAY" (Ordinance No. 353-2020). Frequency: the city's How To Ride page says "Streetcars arrive every 15 minutes"; Visit Cincy says "generally comes by a stop every 20 - 25 minutes". Real-time arrivals: Transit app. "Service animals are allowed on board." Owned by the City of Cincinnati; operated and maintained by Transdev.

Source: cincinnati-oh.gov (opens in a new tab)

Streetcar accessibility

"The Connector is accessible to everyone. It is one of only two systems in the country to feature 100 percent low-floor streetcar vehicles."

Details

"This means the floor of the streetcar is level with the curb, allowing quick access for wheeled vehicles (bicycles, strollers, wheelchairs, scooters and personal shopping carts)." "Audio announcements and visual aids such as Braille and designated seating for individuals with disabilities." "Those who use wheelchairs, scooters and other mobility aids should enter through the center double doors."

Metro (SORTA) buses

Metro is Southwest Ohio’s public transit system, serving Hamilton County residents along with providing commuter service to Clermont, Butler and Warren counties into Cincinnati.

Details

Fares: $2.20 Local Routes; $3.00 Express Routes ("designated with an X"). Transfers free with rides bought in the Transit App, good on up to two buses for 120 minutes. Day Pass: $4.40 Local (24 hours from activation); $6.00 Express; $5.00 Metro/TANK day pass, "Available for purchase onboard the bus". Tap&Save in the Transit app with EZfare caps local fares at $4.40/day. "Cash is always accepted on board; however, you must have exact change." Children under 55 inches ride free with a paying adult (limit two). Sales office: Mercantile Center Arcade, 115 E 5th St, Mon.–Fri. 7:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Customer service (513) 632-7575.

"Red Bike is Cincinnati’s non-profit station-based bike share system."

Details

Pricing (cincyredbike.org/pricing): Two-Hour Pass $12 ("Unlimited 2-hour rides for 24 hours, $5 per additional for rides lasting longer than 2 hours" per the system pricing feed); Monthly Pass $30; Annual Pass $150. BLINK: "Ride all week with the BLINK 1-Week Pass ($20)"; "plan to dock your bike at a Red Bike station before entering the pedestrian festival area"; "Do not ride Red Bikes through BLINK’s pedestrian area or closed streets."
