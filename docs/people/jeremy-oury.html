Overview/People/Jérémy Oury

Photo via BLINK

BLINKArtist

Jérémy Oury

France

Jérémy Oury is a French digital artist and sound designer whose work explores perception, space, and light through immersive audiovisual environments. With a background in sound design for theatre and architectural acoustics, he creates fulldome films, video mapping works, and light installations that investigate the relationship between reality, technology, and consciousness. His practice, presented internationally at festivals and venues such as MUTEK MX, Mapping Festival, ISEA, FILE, Artechouse, and the Paris Planetarium, blends algorithmic visuals and generative soundscapes to construct sensory environments where order and chaos coexist. Through these immersive systems, Oury invites viewers into contemplative experiences that blur the boundaries between digital space and inner perception.
